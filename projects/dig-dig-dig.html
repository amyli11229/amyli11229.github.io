Dig! Dig! Dig!

Brackeys Game Jam 2025.2 Game Design, Pixel Art, Procedural Generation

Design lead on a four-person Brackeys Game Jam team—pixel art direction and player-facing experience for a short procedural digging game.

Players dig for loot under a 120-second timer, then race rising lava. Built with Squiffy121, Nng11, and jamesedra for Brackeys Game Jam 2025.2.

Pixel art

Environment layers, drill variants, loot, and UI assets from the game’s visual direction.

My Approach

  • Led design for a 4-person game jam team
  • Defined the pixel art visual direction and overall look of the game
  • Shaped player-facing experience around digging, combat, and risk-reward decisions
  • Collaborated with teammates on gameplay feel and presentation for a jam submission
