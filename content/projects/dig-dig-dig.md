You have 120 seconds to dig deep, fight monsters, and collect points. When the timer is up, lava starts rising from the bottom of the cave. Play it safe near the surface—or go deeper for better loot.

Built as a submission for Brackeys Game Jam 2025.2 with teammates Squiffy121, Nng11, and jamesedra.
